THE MANIFESTO

A more connected, engaging and student-focused campus — with student ideas at the heart of what we do.

01The Student Voice Initiative

Propose an anonymous feedback form for concerns and suggestions on academics, transport, facilities, events and welfare. Prioritise issues, raise them with the relevant departments and share regular progress updates.

02Getting There, Together

Explore a shuttle between DIC Metro Station and the University by surveying demand and assessing preferred timings, safety, feasibility, providers, costs and approval requirements.

03A Better Start?

Gather student feedback and discuss the current 8:15 AM class start with Faculty and Admin, including possible 8:30, 8:45 or 9:00 starts, while considering timetable and operational constraints.

04Beyond the Classroom

Poll students on destinations and activities, explore affordable options and work towards at least one major educational or recreational outing, subject to feasibility and approval.

05SIUD UNPLUGGED

Propose an annual student-led campus fest with games, competitions, music, dance, open mic, karaoke, talent shows, student-run stalls, art exhibitions and a photobooth.

06More Ways to Get Involved

Encourage interactive sessions, informal get-togethers, skill-development workshops, opportunities to discover student talent and initiatives supporting wellbeing and participation.

07Stronger Student–Faculty Connections

Encourage discussion groups, interactive sessions, academic workshops and guest sessions, while respectfully communicating student concerns to the right people.

08A Council That Works as a Team

Build clear responsibilities, regular meetings, committee coordination, realistic deadlines, accountability, open communication and collective leadership.
